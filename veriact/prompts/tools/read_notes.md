Read one of your note files back. Read-only: it costs no game action and does not end the turn.

`file` — `GUIDE.md` or `WORKING.md`.

Read `GUIDE.md` when what you are seeing does not match what you believe — your standing account of
what the actions do and how things here behave lives there, and a rule you are misremembering is
worse than one you never wrote. Read `WORKING.md` before you commit to this turn's action, to see
what this level actually looks like, what you have already tried on it, and what your plan said to
do next.

Read before you overwrite either file with `write_notes`, every time — `write_notes` replaces the
whole file, so anything you did not send back because you had forgotten it was there is gone.

Read at the start of a new level too, before you rewrite `WORKING.md` for it: `GUIDE.md` is what
tells you which of the last level's findings still apply.
