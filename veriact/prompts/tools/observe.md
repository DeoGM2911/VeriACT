Report what you currently see. Read-only -- it costs no game action and changes nothing.

Call this first, before you verify or plan -- it's the first thing you do every turn, not just
when something changed.

`description` -- what you currently see, tied to the specific objects/features you're already
tracking by their established names (see `write_notes`'s naming convention -- reuse the same
names here, don't invent new ones). If this is your first look at a level, say so plainly ("a
first read") and describe what's in front of you at whatever grain you can make out -- there is
nothing recorded yet to check it against. Otherwise, note anything new, missing, or different from
what you had recorded, at either the whole-level scale (do the overall rules and mechanics still
hold) or in some small local detail easy to miss at a glance.

Look at the whole frame, not just wherever your eye lands first: the corners and edges as well as
the center, and both the large, obvious objects/features and the small ones that are easy to skip
past -- a thin line, a single-pixel marker, a small icon tucked in a corner.

You likely already have something written about this level in `WORKING.md`, from an earlier turn
this level or carried over from a previous one -- `read_notes` on it before you describe what you
see if you're not sure what's already recorded there.

Free to spend a `read_notes`, `inspect`, or `read_pixels` call first if a closer look, or a check
against what you already wrote, would sharpen this. `write_notes` and `play` are not available
here -- writing anything down is a later step's job, and so is acting; this one only looks and
reports.
